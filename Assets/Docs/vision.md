# Vision:
A short 1-2 hour top down story driven game with puzzles about a human that
accidentally enters a magic world and tries to go back to his world following the guidance of a misterious lady.

### What's in scope?
1. Four main explorable areas:
	1. The witch hut.
	2. The forest.
	3. The mountains.
	4. The shores.
2. Dialogue system.
3. Quest system.
2. Simple collectibles system
3. Simple spell casting system
4. Simple enemy AI & behavior

### What is not in scope?
1. Comlpex combat mechanics.
2. Complex physics mechanics.
3. Complex speel leveling system.
4. Complex enemy ai system.
